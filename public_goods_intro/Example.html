Each year, each company starts with €2 million.

Example A: All 4 companies invest €1M
  • 1. You keep €1M from your budget.
  • 2. The group's €4M total is boosted to €6M.
  • 3. Your share of the boosted total is €6M ÷ 4 = €1.5M.
  • Your total: €1M (kept) + €1.5M (shared) = €2.5 million
Example B: You invest €0, others invest €1M
  • 1. You keep your full €2M.
  • 2. The group's €3M total is boosted to €4.5M.
  • 3. Your share of the boosted total is €4.5M ÷ 4 = €1.125M.
  • Your total: €2M (kept) + €1.125M (shared) = €3.125 million
Note: You earn more in this scenario because you benefit from others' investment without spending your own budget.
Example C: You invest €1M, others invest €0
  • 1. You keep €1M from your budget.
  • 2. The group's €1M total is boosted to €1.5M.
  • 3. Your share of the boosted total is €1.5M ÷ 4 = €0.375M.
  • Your total: €1M (kept) + €0.375M (shared) = €1.375 million
Note: You earn less in this scenario because you invest while the other companies do not.
