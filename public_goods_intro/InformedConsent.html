Study on decision-making and cooperation

This study is conducted by researchers from the Tilburg School of Social and Behavioral Sciences and the Tilburg School of Economics and Management at Tilburg University.

The purpose of this study is to investigate how transport companies make investment decisions in shared infrastructure during the transition to more sustainable freight transport, how cooperation strategies evolve over time, and how individual attitudes and characteristics relate to these decisions.

In this study, you will take the role of a transport company that decides how much to invest in a shared electric truck charging infrastructure across multiple rounds. You will make these decisions together with other people, each representing a different company. Your decisions will impact both your own earnings and those of the other participants in your group. {{ if incentives }} The earnings you accumulate during the task will be converted into additional payment at the end of the study. {{ endif }}

{{ if timers }}

Important: In this study, four people will participate in a live, interactive decision task. For this reason, the interactive elements of the task are timed. Each page should be completed within 5 minutes, which is more than enough time. A timer will be displayed on all pages where this applies.

If, during the interactive task, at least one participant does not respond within {{ timeout_time_minutes }} minutes, the session will automatically end for all participants. {{ if incentives }} In that case, all participants will still receive the base participation payment, but no additional earnings from the task will be paid. {{ endif }} Therefore, please make sure that you have enough time available before you decide to participate.

{{ endif }}

Study Procedure and Interaction

The study has several rounds. In each round, you will choose how much of your budget to invest in shared infrastructure. The total investment from the group will be multiplied and then shared equally with everyone, regardless of how much each person invested. The decisions made by you and the other people in your group will together affect how much you earn during the task. The higher your earnings, the higher the additional payment you will receive.

Right before the interactive task begins, you will be asked a few brief questions about your expectations. Then, after the interactive task, you will be asked to complete six short questionnaires about attitudes and individual characteristics. These questionnaires include topics such as general views about trust and sustainability. This part of the study is not interactive and there will be no timer.

{{ if timers }}

Please note that these questionnaires will only appear if the interactive task is completed by all participants in the group. If the interactive session ends due to a timeout, the questionnaire section will not be shown, and the study will end for your entire group.

{{ endif }}

Duration and Payment

The study will take approximately 20 minutes in total. The instruction phase and interactive decision task will take approximately 15 minutes. The questionnaire section will take approximately 5–10 minutes. You will receive a base payment of £3.00 for participating in the study. {{ if incentives }} In addition, you will earn additional payment based on your earnings during the interactive task. All participants who complete the task and questionnaires will receive this additional payment (minimum ~£0.75). {{ endif }}

{{ if incentives }} To receive the full payment (base payment and any additional earnings), participants must complete the interactive task (all rounds) and the subsequent questionnaire section. {{ endif }}

{{ if incentives }} Note on interruption:

  • Interruption during the introductory phase: The introductory phase includes reading instructions and comprehension checks. If you leave or time out during this phase, the interactive session was never initiated. In this case, no payment will be provided.
  • Interruption during matching: If you complete the introductory phase and wait on the grouping page, but a full group of 4 cannot be formed within the 5-minute waiting period, the study will end. In this case, you will still receive the base payment for your time spent waiting.
  • Interruption after grouping: If the interactive task ends early due to a timeout or technical issue after grouping has occurred (regardless of whether the timeout was caused by you or another member), participants will still receive the base payment. However, because the task was not completed fully, no additional earnings from the task will be paid and the questionnaire section will be skipped.

In all cases where a session ends early, it will not be possible to restart or rejoin the study. {{ endif }}

Confidentiality and Questions

All data obtained from participants will be stored in pseudonymized form. Any information collected about you will be coded using a fake name (pseudonym) or initials and numbers, for example abc-123, etc. The information which has your identifiable information will be kept separately from the rest of your data. It will be stored in a password-protected file and only the researchers will have access to it. Once anonymized, the data may be made available to researchers via accessible data repositories and possibly used for novel purposes. The data will be stored for at least 10 years.

This study has been approved by the Ethics Review Board of the Tilburg School of Social and Behavioral Sciences (Department of Social Psychology bulk application: EC_TSB_RP1771_5657_4736). If you have any remarks or complaints regarding this research, you may contact the Ethics Review Board of Tilburg School of Social and Behavioral Sciences via ERB@tilburguniversity.edu.

Participation, Benefits and Risks

Participation in this study is completely voluntary. You have the right to withdraw at any time or refuse to participate entirely. If you wish to withdraw, you can simply close your internet browser. There will be no negative consequences if you withdraw or refuse to participate. However, additional earnings from the task depend on completing both the interactive task and questionnaires. Participants who have entered the interactive task but cannot complete it due to timeouts or technical issues will still receive the base participation payment. Risks are minimal for involvement in this study. It is very unlikely that answering the questions affects you emotionally or otherwise. You have the right, in principle, to request access to and rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data.
