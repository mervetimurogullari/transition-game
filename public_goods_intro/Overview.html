Part 1: Instructions

Part 2: The interactive decision task

You will represent a transport company together with 3 other participants, each representing a different company. Your goal: Manage the transition from diesel to electric trucks.

Right after your group is formed, you will first answer a few brief questions about your expectations.

Each round, you decide how to allocate your budget:

  • Investing in a shared public charging infrastructure
  • Keeping it for your own company

The shared infrastructure grows only if companies invest. However, because it is public, all companies benefit from it even if some companies invest less or not at all.
