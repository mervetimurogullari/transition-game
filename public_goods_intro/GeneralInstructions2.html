1. Task structure
  • Players: You and 3 others (4 companies total).
  • Duration: {{ num_rounds }} rounds (representing {{ num_rounds }} years).
  • Budget: You start each year with €{{ endowment }} million.
  • Decision: Invest any amount of your budget in the shared charging fund, or invest nothing.
2. How benefits grow

To simulate public support and shared infrastructure benefits:

The boost: The total group investment is boosted by 50%. This reflects public support and synergistic gains.
Equal sharing: The boosted total is shared equally among all 4 companies, regardless of how much each company invested.
3. Your final total

At the end of the task, your total earnings consist of:

  • The money you kept from your annual budgets
  • Your share of the boosted total (total group investment plus the 50% public boost)
Remember: You will receive a share of the boosted total even if you do not invest. However, if no one invests, the boosted total remains small and all companies receive a small return.
